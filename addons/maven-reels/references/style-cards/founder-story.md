# Style card: founder story (brand ad)

Learnt from a popular 40 s Arabic app teaser (soft 3D toy town, word-by-word captions). Only the format is copied.
Example built with it: `examples/tannour-ar/reel.html`.

```
FORMAT     9:16 · 35-40 s · one camera that pushes and pans; hard cuts only between places (shop -> home -> shop)
STRUCTURE  1 I opened <place>  2 first customer, simple and happy  3 it got famous  4 crowd grows
           5 it overflows (queue, waiting, angry)  6 a failed fix (paper numbers, a sofa...)  7 so I built <product>
           8 how it works x3 (each one a phone screen change)  9 the moment it pays off (notification)
           10 the happy ending in the place  11 logo card + "coming soon" / link
HOOK       empty street, the place builds itself piece by piece on the first 4 words (wall, window, door, sign)
VOICE      first person owner, local dialect, 3-3.5 words a second, starts at 0.0 s, no music gaps
CAPTIONS   one word at a time, big (≈130 px at 1080 wide), white, heavy rounded Arabic font, soft drop shadow,
           upper third, pops in (scale .6 -> 1) on its word
WORLD      soft toy-like 3D/clay look, daylight sky with drifting clouds, city skyline, palms, a street with a
           pavement; the brand colour on the shop (green there, brown/red in the example)
CHARACTERS round toy people, colourful shirts, about 1/4 of the screen tall when close; they walk, bob, wave
CAMERA     starts wide, pushes in during the build, pans along the queue, small zoom punches on key words
GRAPHICS   an icon dock at the bottom: one icon per story beat lights up when told; a phone mock-up slides up
           from the bottom for the product part, its numbers count live; a notification drops in
PACE       a new event about every 0.8-1.2 s
SOUND      voice on top, light music bed, soft pops/whooshes on build steps; -14 LUFS
END        white rounded card with the logo, name, "coming soon" and the site, over the wide shot
DON'T COPY the original brand, its logo, characters, shop name and script
```
